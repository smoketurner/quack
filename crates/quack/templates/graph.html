Knowledge graph

?How this page works

The knowledge graph is a map of the people, companies, places, and other things in your workspace and how they are connected. Each one is typed by your ontology, and each links back to the document passage or table row it came from, so you can check it.

  1. Set up the ontology first. The graph only holds the kinds of things and relationships the Ontology page defines.
  2. Extract. Extract fills the graph from your mapped tables (instant, no AI) and has the AI read your documents (it shows how many passages first, and asks).
  3. Explore. Type a name to see what it connects to, or pick a kind to list everything of that kind. Path finds how two things are linked. Click a dot to see its details and sources.
  4. Clean up. When the same thing appears under two names, it shows up in Merge review: merge it or keep both.

A provisional banner means the ontology was accepted without review; questions in query mode ignore the graph until someone marks it reviewed. A stale banner means the ontology changed since the last extraction: Revalidate drops what no longer fits, after listing it, and extracting again adds what is new. To rename a class or relation without losing its nodes and edges, use Rename on the Ontology page.

Entities typed by the ontology's classes, relations between them, and for every node and edge the document chunk or table row it came from.
