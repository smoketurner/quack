Ontology

?How this page works

The ontology is the list of kinds of things your workspace talks about (vendors, shipments, countries), the relationships between them (a vendor ships to a country), and the facts each kind has (a vendor's founding year). The knowledge graph is built from it, and the assistant uses it to answer questions about how things connect.

  1. Get a first draft. Propose turns your tables into kinds, facts, and relationships (no AI involved). Check also from the documents to have the AI read a sample of your documents too; that runs in the background. With no tables yet, Install the built-in ontology gives you a general starting point.
  2. Review the suggestions. Each one waits in the Review queue. Select the ones that make sense and press Accept selected, or Reject selected for the rest. Suggestions marked low support were seen in only a few documents; accept those you know to be right.
  3. Build the graph. Once the ontology looks right, go to the Graph page and extract.

Every accepted change makes a new version; you can compare versions and restore an older one at any time. Each property's Edit meaning sets the description, unit, and synonyms shown beside every column mapped to it, and the Measures forms add, change, and remove named calculations. To edit everything at once, use Edit as JSON. To give a class or relation a new id, use Rename: the graph's nodes and edges move with it. Changing an id in the JSON instead removes the old id, and revalidating the graph then drops its nodes and edges.

The schema of the knowledge graph: classes with single inheritance from entity, relations with a domain and a range, typed properties, and table mappings. Every accepted change is a new version.
