# Episode Narrative: Storm Report SR-7765

Storm Report SR-7765 covers a hurricane landfall near Escambia County, Florida, on
September 16 with sustained winds of 105 miles per hour, a Category 2 storm on the
Saffir-Simpson scale. One direct fatality was recorded from storm surge. Property
damage was estimated at 62.00M. The forecast office of record was MOB.
