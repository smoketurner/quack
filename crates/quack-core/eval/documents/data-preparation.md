# Storm Data Preparation Guidance

Guidance for what may be entered into the storm events database and how.

## Purpose

This guidance defines what constitutes a stormy event worth recording, how each
element is measured, and how deaths, injuries, and damage are attributed to it. It exists
so that entries filed by different forecast offices stay comparable.

## What May Be Entered

An entry requires a recognized event type, a begin and end time, a county or public
forecast zone, and the forecast office that filed it. An event with no measurable
impact and no unusual character is not entered.

## Direct Versus Indirect

A death or injury is direct when the storm's physical force caused it: floodwater,
falling trees, flying debris, lightning strike. It is indirect when it followed the
event without physical force being the cause: a heart attack while clearing storm
debris, a traffic accident on a road made icy by a winter storm, carbon monoxide
poisoning from a generator run during a power outage.
