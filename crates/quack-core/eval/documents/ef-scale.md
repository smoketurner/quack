# The Enhanced Fujita Scale

The Enhanced Fujita Scale rates tornado intensity from the damage a tornado left behind,
estimating the three-second gust wind speed that would produce it.

## EF0

EF0 tornadoes have estimated wind speeds of 65 to 85 miles per hour. Damage is light:
some shingles removed from roofs, gutters or siding damaged, branches broken off trees.

## EF1

EF1 tornadoes have estimated wind speeds of 86 to 110 miles per hour. Damage is
moderate: roofs severely stripped, mobile homes overturned or badly damaged, exterior
doors lost.

## EF2

EF2 tornadoes have estimated wind speeds of 111 to 135 miles per hour. Damage is
considerable: roofs torn off well-built houses, mobile homes completely destroyed, large
trees snapped or uprooted.

## EF3

EF3 tornadoes have estimated wind speeds of 136 to 165 miles per hour. Damage is severe:
entire stories of well-built houses destroyed, heavy cars lifted off the ground and
thrown.

## EF4

EF4 tornadoes have estimated wind speeds of 166 to 200 miles per hour. Damage is
devastating: well-built houses leveled, structures with weak foundations blown away.

## EF5

EF5 tornadoes have estimated wind speeds over 200 miles per hour. Damage is
incredible: strong frame houses swept away, steel-reinforced concrete structures
critically damaged.
