Field Office Readiness Review

The readiness board reviews every forecast office once a year against the storm season checklist.

Spotter network

An office passes the spotter review when at least ninety percent of its registered spotters completed the annual refresher, which the Wakefield office met with ninety-four percent.

Backup power

Every office must hold seventy-two hours of generator fuel on site; the Glasgow office recorded forty hours and was marked deficient.
