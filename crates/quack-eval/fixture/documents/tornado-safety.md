# Tornado Safety

When a tornado warning is issued, move immediately to the lowest floor of a sturdy
building, away from windows, and put as many walls as possible between yourself and the
outside. A basement or an interior room such as a bathroom or closet on the lowest floor
offers the best protection. Mobile homes, even those tied down, offer little protection
and should be abandoned for a sturdy building or a designated shelter. If caught
outdoors with no shelter available, lie flat in a low spot such as a ditch and cover
your head with your hands.
